## Brand & Style

The design system bridges academic prestige with media-forward digital dynamism. Built specifically for high-intensity competition workflows, it balances the institution's historic credibility with real-time judging speed and responsiveness.

### Target Audience & Persona
- **Jury & Judges:** Faculty, industry experts, and guest evaluators operating primarily on tablets and mobile screens in dynamic grading environments.
- **Contest Organizers & Coordinators:** Academic admins managing multi-stage round progression, time quotas, and score moderation.
- **Contestants & Observers:** Students and live audiences tracking leaderboard standings and submission timelines.

### Emotional Response & Principles
- **Authoritative & Reliable:** The visual weight of collegiate blue fosters an environment of fairness, institutional integrity, and decisive grading.
- **High-Velocity Kinetic:** Cyan accents and dynamic live badges bring energy, live-scoring urgency, and modern media appeal.
- **Clarity Under Pressure:** Uncluttered layouts, instant touch targets, and unambiguous numerical scoring feedback minimize cognitive load in fast-paced evaluation rooms.

### Design Movement: Modern Corporate Utility with Media Accents
The aesthetic avoids sterile administrative interfaces by combining structured institutional utility (structured forms, strict grids, high-contrast borders) with modern fluid glassmorphism hints and vibrant neon cyan focal points for live indicators.

## Colors

The color palette is rooted in institutional trust, accented by high-impact media highlights and strictly calibrated semantic states.

### Primary Role Hierarchy
- **Primary Blue (`#034EA2`):** Primary branding anchor, main CTA buttons, top-level navigation, active progress nodes, and major confirmations.
- **Secondary Blue (`#2073B7`):** Hover interactions, secondary controls, active tab indicators, and structured table headers.
- **Media Cyan (`#00D9FF`):** Reserved strictly for high-visibility visual anchors—active/live session indicators, pulse dots, selected grade highlights, and high-performance metrics. Paired with `#152B3B` text when used as a fill to maintain accessibility.
- **Brand Red (`#ED1C24`):** Institutional secondary accent applied sparingly for critical actions, time-sensitive round deadlines, rejected submissions, and urgent organizer alerts.
- **App Canvas Background (`#F6F9FC`):** A soft, cool-tinted neutral surface that reduces glare during prolonged judging sessions while keeping cards crisp.
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`):** Base for scoring cards, sticky sheets, interactive dialogs, and rubric containers.
- **Text Main (`#152B3B`):** Deep navy slate delivering AAA contrast against pure white and AA contrast against the application background.

### Semantic Tones
- **Success (`#10B981`):** Submitted scores, verified rubrics, pass states.
- **Warning (`#F59E0B`):** Unsaved changes, draft status, nearing round deadline.
- **Error (`#EF4444`):** Disqualification, score variance threshold exceeded, missing mandatory fields.
- **Info (`#3B82F6`):** General system notifications, judging instructions, stage metadata.

## Typography

The typography system relies solely on **Be Vietnam Pro**, engineered to deliver flawless legibility for Vietnamese diacritics, tonal marks, and condensed alphanumeric contest codes across viewport scales down to 320px.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Display & Headlines:** Used for competition rounds, team names, and aggregate stage summaries. Scaled downward on viewports below 480px to prevent text wraps on diacritic-dense Vietnamese terms.
- **Body:** Calibrated line heights (1.4x to 1.5x) prevent accent clashes between lines in long-form submission feedback, project abstracts, and judging criterion descriptions.
- **Labels & Tags:** Uppercase tracking (`0.04em`) reserved for status badges (e.g., `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `LIVE`) to maximize quick-glance decipherability on small viewports.
- **Numeric Score Role:** A specialized tabular numeric variant ensures rapid entry and comparison of dynamic grading totals inside rubric tiles and persistent bottom sheets.

## Layout & Spacing

A strict Mobile-First fluid grid system designed for effortless one-handed thumb interaction on compact phones (360px–390px) that smoothly adapts into structured multi-pane workspaces on desktop dashboards.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid. Content margins fixed at `1rem` (16px) with an ultra-compact `0.75rem` (12px) margin fallback for 320px devices. Forms and scoring actions stack vertically into single-column ribbons.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Split-pane layout activates: contestant submission material pins to the left 4 columns, while dynamic rubric scoring scrolls on the right 4 columns.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout max-width `1280px` centered. Left navigation column (3 cols), evaluation content pane (5 cols), real-time live scorecard & judging chat stream (4 cols).

### Spacing Strategy
- Form inputs, slider steps, and score increment buttons observe a strict **8pt rhythmic stepping** with a minimum touch zone boundary of 44px by 44px.
- Component padding adheres strictly to `space-sm` (8px) for badges/chips, `space-md` (16px) for standard scoring inputs and rubric bodies, and `space-lg` (24px) for master card enclosures.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated via clean structural boundaries layered with ambient soft shadows to preserve responsiveness on varied mobile chipsets.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Surface):** Canvas background (`#F6F9FC`). Used for outer view margins and inactive tab bars.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Rubric criteria cards, criteria headers, and form inputs. Styled with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient tinted drop: `0px 2px 6px rgba(3, 78, 162, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Evaluator Elements):** Currently active rubric criterion card, dropdown menus, and hover states. Border shifts to `rgba(3, 78, 162, 0.25)` accompanied by `0px 8px 20px rgba(3, 78, 162, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Sticky Sheets):** Persistent mobile scoring bottom bar, modal sheets, and quick score dialers. Elevated with `0px -4px 24px rgba(21, 43, 59, 0.12)` along with an upper hairline stroke `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
- **Level 4 (Floating Live Alerts):** Toast notifications, real-time sync drop-downs, and round warnings. Styled with `0px 12px 32px rgba(21, 43, 59, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined modern curvature balance (`roundedness: 2`). This keeps containers structural, clean, and professional while ensuring touch-friendly items feel ergonomic and inviting.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Standard UI Elements (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Text fields, rubric card outer boundaries, standard buttons, submission preview thumbnails, and dropdown selectors.
- **Container Elements (`1rem` / 16px):** Dialog boxes, bottom sheet sheets, candidate profile cards, and rubric section summaries.
- **Pill Shapes (`9999px`):** Status badges (`LIVE`, `SUBMITTED`), round phase chips, step indicator nodes, and floating quick-action pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** `#034EA2` background, pure white text, 8px corner radius. Minimum height 48px on mobile (44px on desktop) for touch-target compliance. Active press scales down slightly (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Button:** Surface white background, 1.5px border `#2073B7`, `#2073B7` text.
- **Live / Urgent Action:** `#ED1C24` background with white text, reserved for "Finalize & Lock Score", "Disqualify", or "End Round".
- **Ghost Action:** Borderless, `#2073B7` typography with `space-sm` internal padding for secondary rubric actions.

### Status Badges
Pill-shaped with a font size of 11px, bold weight, and uppercase tracking.
- **LIVE:** Subtle Cyan glow, `#00D9FF` tint (15% opacity) background, `#034EA2` text, accompanied by an animated pulsing dot.
- **DRAFT:** Neutral slate background (`#E2E8F0`), `#152B3B` text.
- **SUBMITTED:** Success emerald background (`#ECFDF5`), `#065F46` text.
- **WAITING:** Warning amber background (`#FFFBEB`), `#92400E` text.
- **REOPENED:** Brand red background (`#FEF2F2`), `#991B1B` text.

### Rubric Criterion Cards
- Independent card units per judging criterion.
- Features criterion title (`title-md`), weight percentage badge (e.g., `30%`), descriptive scoring guide, and an inline score adjuster.
- When focused, the card displays a 2px left border in `#034EA2` with a subtle elevation shift.

### Score Inputs & Steppers
- Rapid evaluation requires dual entry: an interactive smooth step slider combined with physical `-` and `+` touch increments (44x44px target) flanking a direct-entry numeric input.
- Numeric display utilizes the bold tabular `numeric-score` token.

### Sticky Scoring Footer (Mobile)
- Docked persistently at the bottom of the viewport (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` respected).
- Layout: Left side displays running total and max score (`88.5 / 100`). Right side features a full-width or dominant primary action button ("Tiếp tục" or "Nộp điểm").
- Background: Surface white with a 1px top border and Level 3 elevation shadow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20px hit surface with 44px invisible tap targets.
- Radio buttons feature a high-contrast `#034EA2` inner ring with smooth spring transitions. Checkbox checks are crisp pure white on `#034EA2` background.
